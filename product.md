# Product

CourseCairn: Build better training programmes with data.

AI training evaluation software that analyzes assessment outcomes and course feedback to identify ineffective learning modules.

- Founder and CEO: Hamzat Ibrahim
- Co-founder and CTO: Victoria Okeke
- Industry: Education, Human Resources, Business Intelligence
- Customers: Training providers, vocational schools and online learning companies
- Core features: Assessment analysis, learning gap discovery, feedback classification, improvement recommendations, course dashboards
- MVP: Assessment imports and course performance reporting
- Planned AWS: Bedrock, SageMaker AI, S3, DynamoDB, Lambda
- Business model: Learner and course-based subscriptions
- Main action: Request a pilot
