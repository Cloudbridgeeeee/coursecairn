How a CourseCairn pilot runs

  1. Import the assessment scores

    Upload a term of scores and feedback from your LMS or spreadsheets.

  2. Read the course report

    Within 48 hours the report ranks modules by pass rate and lists the weak questions.

  3. Approve the changes

    Course leads review each recommendation and track the next cohort’s scores.

What training teams ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

Which assessment files can we import?

Exports from Moodle, Canvas or any LMS, and spreadsheets of scores and comments.

Does it change learner grades?

No. It reads scores and suggests course changes; course leads decide.

What does a course subscription cost?

Priced by active learners and courses. We quote after the pilot.

Are learner names needed?

No. Learner IDs are enough, and names can be removed before upload.

Can online learning companies use it?

Yes. It works for self-paced and cohort courses alike.

Which courses suit a pilot?

A course with at least one finished term of assessments and some written learner feedback.
